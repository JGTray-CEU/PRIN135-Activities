I Tried the "Top 10 Things To Do in America"

Ryan Trahan and Haley embark on a cross-country adventure, attempting to visit the top ten tourist sites in the United States within ten days. Along the way, the duo documents their experiences, manages travel logistics, and captures memories in a custom scrapbook to determine which iconic destinations provide the most rewarding visitor experiences.

We Should Not Have Played This Game...

UNCS and friends return to play Pico Park 2, navigating a series of increasingly difficult cooperative levels. The group must rely on teamwork, communication, and precise coordination to solve puzzles, dodge obstacles, and face off against challenging bosses while attempting to clear each stage.

Last To Leave Mansion, Keeps It

Participants who publicly criticized MrBeast's challenges are put to the test in a high-stakes competition. Throughout their stay in a massive mansion, they must endure isolation, physical limitations, and various obstacles to prove their claims. They navigate complex social dynamics and unforeseen challenges while vying for ultimate ownership of the property.
