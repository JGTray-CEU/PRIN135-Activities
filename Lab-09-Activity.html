Spider-man: Brand New Day

Following the events of No Way Home, Peter Parker navigates a fresh start in New York City after Doctor Strange's spell wiped the world's memory of his identity. Stripped of Stark technology and completely on his own, Peter balances college life and strict anonymity while protecting the city as a ground-level vigilante against emerging street threats.
